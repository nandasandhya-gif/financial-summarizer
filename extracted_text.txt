ABSTRACT 
The project "AI -Based Meeting Minutes Generator" proposes the development of an intelligent system designed to 
automatically generate concise and accurate summaries of meeting conversations by leveraging advanced 
Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques. The system captures audio r ecordings of meetings and transcribes 
them into text, after which it employs a combination of NLP methods such as speaker diarization, keyword extraction, 
text segmentation,  and abstractive summarization to identify key topics, important discussions, decis ions, and 
actionable items. The model is trained to filter out irrelevant dialogue, comprehend contextual information, and extract 
critical insights from extended conversations.  The resulting meeting minutes are structured coherently and can be 
exported in various user-friendly formats to facilitate documentation and distribution. By automating the minute-
taking process, this approach aims to reduce manual effort, minimize errors, and enhance organizational 
productivity through efficient and reliable meeting documentation.

1. INTRODUCTION 
In today’s professional, academic, and organizational environments, meetings play a vital role in decision-
making, planning, and collaboration. However, traditional manual minute -taking often suffers from errors, 
inconsistencies, delays,  and administrative overhead. With the rapid growth of digital and hybrid meetings, 
organizations face increasing difficulties in maintaining accurate, standardized, and easily retrievable records. Manual 
note-taking depends heavily on individual note -takers whose subjective interpretations and varying writing styles can 
lead to information loss, especially during fast -paced discussions. Delayed documentation further hinders 
communication clarity, project progress, and compliance. To address these challenges, this project proposes an 
automated meeting minutes ge neration and management system  built using Python. The system transforms raw 
meeting inputs such as transcripts, agendas, and discussion notes into professionally formatted, structured, and 
standardized meeting minutes. By integrating advanced text process ing techniques, formatting algorithms, and 
automated document generation modules, the system produces outputs in common formats like Microsoft Word 
(DOCX) and PDF. This approach reduces reliance on the recorder’s experience or writing  skills, ensuring accuracy, 
consistency, and efficiency in meeting documentation. Ultimately, the solution offers organizations  a scalable, 
error-resistant framework that bridges conventional manual practices and modern automated tools to enhance 
meeting management and documentation processes.In today’s professional, academic, and organizational

2. METHODOLOGY 
The methodology of the Automated Meeting Minutes Generation and Management System is structured as a multi  -
stage pipeline to ensure accurate, consistent, and efficient documentation of meetings. The process begins with the 
collection of raw meeting data, which can be audio recordings or text transcripts. Audio inputs are converted into te xt 
using speech  -to- text conversion tools, providing a foundational transcript for further processing.Subsequently, 
speaker diarization is performed to identify and attribute each segment of speech to the corresponding participant, 
enhancing the clarity and structure of the minutes. The transcript then undergoes text preprocessing, where noise, filler 
words, and irrelevant content are removed, and the text is normalized for uniformity.

5. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS 
The Automated Meeting Minutes Generation and Management System requires both hardware and software 
components to ensure efficient and reliable operation. For hardware, a minimum of 4 GB RAM and 10 GB disk space 
is recommended, along  with a microphone for real-time audio input if needed. A multi-core processor is 
advised for handling computationally intensive tasks such as speech-to-text conversion and NLP processing. 
For software, the system is built using Python (version 3.11 or higher) and relies on libraries such as Flask for 
backend web services, python-docx for DOCX document generation, and PyPDF2 for PDF export. Additional

6. IMPLEMENTATION 
The implementation of the Automated Meeting Minutes Generation and Management System involves integrating 
multiple modules to process meeting data and generate structured minutes. The system is developed in Python and 
utilizes a Flask- based web interface for user interaction. Audio inputs are processed using speech -to-text APIs such as 
OpenAI Whisper or Google Speech API, converting spoken words into text transcripts. Speaker diarization is 
performed using specialized  libraries to attribute each utterance to the correct participant, enhancing clarity in 
the generated minutes . Text preprocessing  modules clean and normalize the transcript, removing filler words 
and irrelevant content. Keyword extraction, topic modeling, sentiment analysis, and entity recognition are carried out 
using NLP libraries like spaCy and transformers. Abstractive summarization models, such as BART or T5, generate 
concise meeting summaries. Action item detection algorithms identify  tasks and responsibilities, which are then listed 
in the  minutes. The final output is formatted and exported in DOCX and PDF formats using python -docx and 
PyPDF2, ensuring compatibility with common office applications.

7. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION 
The implementation of the Automated Meeting Minutes Generation and Management System resulted in substantial 
improvements in the accuracy, efficiency, and consistency of meeting documentation. The performance of the 
proposed model was evaluated using stand ard NLP and information extraction metrics, including precision, recall, F1 -
score, ROUGE, and BLEU. The system achieved consistently high precision and recall scores, confirming that the 
extracted keywords, topics, and action items were not only relevant b ut also sufficiently comprehensive for complete 
meeting representation. 
The abstractive summarization module generated concise and contextually coherent summaries that closely resembled 
manually written minutes prepared by experienced professional note-takers. This was validated by strong ROUGE and 
BLEU values, demonstrating that the automated summaries preserved semantic meaning, intent, and task-
specific information. Furthermore, the integration of sentiment analysis and named entity recognition contributed 
valuable contextual depth to meeting records by identifying emotional cues and highlighting key stakeholders within 
discussions. The action -item detection component performed effectively in identifying tasks, due dates, and 
responsible stakeholders, ensuring that actionable decisions were systematically emphasized within the minutes. User

8. CONCLUSION 
The Automated Meeting Minutes Generation and Management System successfully addresses  the challenges 
associated with traditional manual minute -taking by providing a scalable, accurate, and efficient solution for meeting 
documentation. By leveraging advanced NLP techniques such as speech -to-text conversion, speaker diarization, 
keyword extraction, topic modeling, sentiment analysis, entity recognition, and abstractive summarizat ion, the system 
ensures that meeting minutes are consistently structured, contextually relevant, and free from subjective biases  . The 
implementation demonstrates significant improvements in documentation accuracy and efficiency, reducing manual 
effort and minimizing errors. The  system’s modular architecture allows for easy integration  with existing 
organizational tools and supports future enhancements, such as improved handling of noisy  audio and real -time 
feedback. Overall, the project offers a robust framework for automated meeting management, enhancing productivity 
and reliability in professional, academic, and organizational settings.
